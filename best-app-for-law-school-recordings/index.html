Law school lectures can have long pauses. The professor reads from the casebook, waits between Socratic questions or gives students time to find a page.

5cut finds these silent parts on your iPhone and cuts them. Then transcribe the recording, identify the speakers and export your notes.

What 5cut does for law students

  • Cut the silence: the speech stays at normal speed.
  • Follow the Socratic method: with Premium, speaker identification separates the professor from the students.
  • Find case names: transcribe the lecture, then search the transcript for a case citation.
  • Keep discussions private: hypothetical client cases and case discussions stay on your iPhone.
  • Prepare for exams: process the lectures of a semester in one batch before finals.

The Socratic method problem

In law school, the most important content often comes in dialogue. The professor asks a question and waits 10 seconds. A student answers, and there is a 5-second pause. Then the professor explains the legal principle.

5cut leaves short pauses alone and cuts the long ones. You choose how much it cuts: Gentle, Moderate or Aggressive.

The law school workflow

  1. Record in class: use the recorder in 5cut, or import an audio file from Files.
  2. Cut the silence: start with Moderate. If 5cut cuts too much, choose Gentle.
  3. Transcribe: make a full transcript with timestamps. With Premium, add speaker labels.
  4. Export: save the shorter recording for your trip to campus, or send the transcript to your outline tool.

Privacy and professional responsibility

Law school classes discuss hypothetical client cases, real case facts and legal strategy. Your recordings never leave your iPhone. 5cut cuts, transcribes and makes notes on the device. There is no 5cut server.
